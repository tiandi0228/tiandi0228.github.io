KoiFlow 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 1 日

KoiFlow 是一款 macOS 桌面鱼池应用。本政策说明应用如何处理信息。

应用处理的信息

根据当前版本的实现，KoiFlow 不要求创建账户，不包含广告或第三方分析 SDK，也不会将个人资料发送到由开发者运营的服务器。应用偏好设置（例如语言、季节、显示模式和动画选项）保存在你的 Mac 本机；桌面应用与屏幕保护程序可能通过 macOS App Group 在本机共享高级版授权状态。

购买

高级版购买和恢复购买由 Apple StoreKit 处理。应用会读取 StoreKit 提供的交易与授权状态，以启用已购买的功能。Apple 对购买信息的处理受 Apple 自己的隐私政策约束。

系统权限与登录项

如果你启用开机启动，macOS 会按你的选择注册或移除 KoiFlow 登录项。应用的桌面显示、鼠标互动及屏幕保护程序行为由你在 macOS 或应用菜单中的设置控制。

数据保留与删除

应用设置保存在本机，可通过应用设置或移除应用及其本机数据来删除。购买记录由 Apple 管理；如需处理 Apple 账户或购买记录，请联系 Apple。

儿童隐私

KoiFlow 不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

政策变更与联系

政策更新将在此页面发布。隐私问题请通过 KoiFlow 的 App Store 支持页面联系开发者。

KoiFlow Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

KoiFlow is a macOS desktop pond app. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Information handled by the app

As implemented in the current version, KoiFlow does not require an account and contains no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. It does not send personal profile information to a developer-operated server. App preferences (such as language, season, display mode, and animation options) are stored locally on your Mac. The desktop app and screen saver may share premium entitlement state locally through a macOS App Group.

Purchases

Premium purchases and restore operations are handled by Apple StoreKit. The app reads transaction and entitlement status provided by StoreKit to enable purchased features. Apple's handling of purchase information is governed by Apple's own privacy policy.

System services and login item

If you enable launch at login, macOS registers or removes the KoiFlow login item according to your choice. Desktop display, mouse interaction, and screen saver behavior are controlled by your settings in macOS or the app menu.

Retention and deletion

App preferences are stored locally and can be removed through app settings or by removing the app and its local data. Apple manages purchase records; contact Apple for requests concerning your Apple Account or purchase history.

Children's privacy

KoiFlow does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

Policy updates will be posted on this page. For privacy questions, contact the developer through KoiFlow's App Store support page.
