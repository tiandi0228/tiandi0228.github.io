KoiFlow 支持

感谢使用 KoiFlow。常见问题：

鱼池没有显示或动画暂停

请确认菜单栏中的「显示鱼池」和「鱼群运动」已启用；如果使用桌面模式，也可尝试从菜单切换到预览窗口再切回。

高级版购买或恢复购买

请使用菜单中的「解锁高级版…」开始购买，或选择「恢复高级版购买」恢复同一 Apple 账户的既有购买。购买由 Apple 处理。

KoiFlow Support

Thanks for using KoiFlow. For common issues:

The pond is hidden or animation is paused

Check that “Show Pond” and “Fish Movement” are enabled in the menu bar menu. In desktop mode, you can also switch to Preview and then switch back.

Premium purchase or restore

Choose “Unlock Premium…” in the menu to purchase, or “Restore Premium Purchase” to restore an existing purchase made with the same Apple Account. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Contact the developer

Support email: hi@syc.im

Include your macOS version, KoiFlow version, and steps to reproduce. Do not send passwords or payment information.
